Permissions Center

Users can see at any time:

  • Which apps have which permissions
  • When each permission was last used
  • Full history of privileged actions per app
  • Why a permission was requested (linked to original prompt)
  • One-tap revocation of any permission
  • Automatic suggestions to revoke unused permissions

Transparency vs Competitors

Unlike iOS/Android where apps operate opaquely:

  • Every capability use is logged and visible
  • Every AI action is attributed and explainable
  • Full source code of every generated app is inspectable
  • Users can see exactly why an action was recommended
  • Trust is earned through transparency, not demanded through opacity
