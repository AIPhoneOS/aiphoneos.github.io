Existing AI App Generators

What already does "describe app, get app" -- and what they get wrong

"Describe an app, get an app" is not a new idea. Multiple products already do this on the web. The question is: why does the world need another one, and why does it need to be an OS?

What They All Get Wrong

1. They generate web apps that live in a browser tab

Every existing AI app generator produces a web app that runs in a browser. It can't access your camera without a permission popup. It can't scan Bluetooth devices. It can't read your contacts or calendar. It can't send an SMS. It can't run in the background. It's a website, not an app.

2. They require a development environment

Bolt.new, Replit, and v0 are designed for developers. They output React, Next.js, or framework-heavy code that requires npm, a build step, and deployment configuration. A non-technical user cannot use these tools to get a working app on their phone.

3. They don't install on your device

The generated app lives on someone else's server. If Bolt.new goes down, your app goes down. If you stop paying Replit, your app disappears. None of these tools produce something that's installed locally on your device, works offline, and belongs to you.

4. They lock you into frameworks

v0 locks you into Next.js. Lovable locks you into React + Supabase. Replit uses proprietary deployment. The generated code is framework-heavy and hard to understand or modify manually.

The Real Differentiator

Bolt.new and friends prove that AI can generate functional web apps. That's validated. The open question is NOT "can AI build apps?" It's "can AI-built apps replace your phone's app store?"

That requires: hardware access, offline operation, local data ownership, installation as first-class apps, and a trust/permission model. No existing product does any of these. That's why this needs to be an OS.
