AIPhone OS

An AI-native operating system for phones and tablets

A new operating system where users create, customise and operate personal software through conversation. Web apps are the only app format. The AI is the primary interaction layer. The device creates software for its owner.

The Core Promise

Software becomes something the device creates for its owner, not something the owner downloads from a store.

Example Interactions

"Create an app that records my child's swimming times, plots improvement and reminds me before meets."

The OS generates a fully functional web app with timer input, chart view, calendar integration, and push notifications. Installed in seconds. Works on phone and tablet.

"Make a dashboard showing my calendar, urgent messages, and this week's expenses."

A personal dashboard pulling from your calendar and contacts (with permission). Runs locally, works offline, adapts to phone or tablet screen.

"Build a Bluetooth scanner that finds nearby devices and logs signal strength over time."

A web app with full BLE access — something no browser would ever allow. The OS Capability Broker makes it safe and audited.

What It Is Not

  • Not an Android launcher or skin
  • Not a Capacitor/WebView wrapper app
  • Not a chatbot sitting on top of a normal phone
  • Not an LLM pretending to be a kernel
  • Not a PWA platform limited by browser restrictions

Why This Must Be an OS

A standalone app cannot deliver this vision. It needs platform-level ownership of:

  • Home screen and primary interaction model
  • App installation, lifecycle, and sandboxing
  • Deep hardware access (BLE, NFC, USB, raw sockets, sensors)
  • Inter-app orchestration and IPC
  • Permission enforcement and audit logging
  • Background execution and power management
  • Secure boot, updates, and recovery

Only an OS can make web apps more powerful than native apps. See Why No Native Apps and Capability Tiers for the full argument.

Offline & Local-First

For a phone OS, offline is not optional. Core functions and already-installed generated apps must work without network. Local AI handles intent routing and simple tasks offline; cloud models handle complex generation only when available and permitted.

Phones and Tablets

The OS targets both smartphones and tablets. Generated apps use responsive web layout to adapt automatically. The AI home experience adapts to screen size — conversational on phone, dashboard-oriented on tablet.
